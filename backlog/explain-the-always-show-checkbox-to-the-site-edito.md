Most 2026 events have Always Show ticked, so past concerts (10, 21, 31 Oct 2026) still show under Upcoming. Past events are now hidden in the browser automatically, so the box is only needed for events that should stay after their date. Consider clearer CMS hint text, or unticking it on past events.
